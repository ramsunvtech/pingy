Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Steppy ("the app") is an open-source goal-tracking application designed to help users build better habits through activities, daily scoring, and rewards. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how Steppy handles your data.

1. Information We Collect

Steppy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Goals and goal periods (e.g., 1 Feb 2026 – 10 Feb 2026)
  • Activity types (e.g., wake up at 5:00 AM, sleep at 10:30 PM)
  • Difficulty levels assigned to activities
  • Daily activity scores and completion status
  • Goal progress, average score, and total score
  • Reward/prize definitions (e.g., 95% first prize, 85% second prize)

All data is stored locally using on-device storage and is never uploaded or shared.

2. Notifications

Steppy uses local notifications to remind you to update your daily activity scores.

  • Notifications are generated entirely on your device
  • No notification data is sent to any server
  • You can disable notifications at any time via your device settings

3. Data Sharing

Steppy does not share your data with:

  • Third-party services
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising networks
  • Government or external organizations

4. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, you are in full control of your information. Deleting the app will permanently remove all stored data.

5. Children’s Privacy

Steppy is safe for users under the age of 13:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No accounts or sign-ups are required
  • No online interaction or data transmission occurs

6. Open Source Transparency

Steppy is an open-source project. The full source code is publicly available on GitHub, allowing anyone to verify how data is handled.

7. Changes to This Policy

If Steppy introduces new features that affect privacy (such as cloud sync or analytics), this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the project’s GitHub repository.
